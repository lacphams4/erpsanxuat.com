TRUMBO - Privacy Policy

App: TRUMBO (com.erpsanxuat.trumbo) · Updated: 3 October 2026

Summary

TRUMBO is a free game with no ads and no in-app purchases. The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Data on your device

Game progress (unlocked parts, current stage, number of players, chosen language and the heroes' names you type) is stored only inside the app on your device. Android may include it in your own Google account backup; the developer cannot access it.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions: no internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage. The whole game is built in and works offline.

Children

The game is suitable for children. Because it collects no data, it collects no information from children in any form. There are no ads, no external links and no chat features.
